The Encoding Rules

Disconnected Graphs

Root's children are component encodings directly:

2K₂ = ((o) (o))
Two K₂ components as siblings

Connected Graphs

Single child of root contains the structure wrapped:

P₃ = (((o) (o)))
One child containing block-cut tree
Why no collisions? Connected and disconnected graphs have different tree depths:
  • Disconnected: Structure at depth 1 (multiple siblings)
  • Connected: Structure at depth 2 (wrapped once more)

Path vs Star Topology

For connected graphs with multiple blocks (cut vertices), topology matters:

Path Topology (P₄)

Blocks nested: ((B₁) ((B₂) (B₃)))

3 blocks in a chain → 2 cut vertices

Matula: 347

Star Topology (K₁,₃)

Blocks flat: ((B₁) (B₂) (B₃))

3 blocks at center → 1 cut vertex

Matula: 103
